About Amaxx

A local name you can rely on

Amaxx Plumbing And Gasfitting is owner-operated by Glenn Perry, a fully licensed plumber and gasfitter based in Tallawang. Since 2015 Glenn has looked after the plumbing and gas needs of homes, farms and businesses right across the central-west — the same person quoting the job is the one who turns up and does it. It's a mobile service, so the ute comes to you, and Glenn's a proud supporter of local clubs and community events around the district.
